System Methodology

End-to-End 8-Step Waste Lifecycle

How waste moves from household doorsteps to segregation centers, recycling organizations, and reward redemption.

1. Register Profile

Household creates an account and sets up collection address with GPS coordinates.

2. Upload Photo

User uploads a reference photo of the waste batch for worker clarity.

3. Schedule Request

Select waste category (Organic, Recyclable, Hazardous, Non-Recyclable) and time window.

4. Worker Accepts

An assigned pickup worker reviews request details and accepts the collection route.

5. Waste Collection

Worker arrives at doorstep, collects segregated bags, and marks status as Collected.

6. Center Delivery

Waste is transported to the nearest recovery & segregation facility (At Center).

7. Weighed & Segregated

Materials weighed by sub-type (Plastic, Metal, Glass, Paper, Bio-waste) and logged.

8. Recycling & Rewards

Recyclables routed to partner plants; household credited with reward coupon points!

Role Portals

Five Coordinated Portals

Household Portal
Resident Interface

Schedule pickups, upload photos, live visual progression tracker (Requested → Segregated), and browse the reward points coupon marketplace.

Pickup Worker App
Collection Operations

View assigned household pickups, one-click status transitions (Accept, Collect, Deliver to Center), and track daily collection volume.

Collection Center
Recovery Facility

Weigh and record segregated streams (Plastic, Metal, Glass, Paper, Bio). Triggers automated household reward computation and material dispatch.

Recycler Organization
Downstream Processing

Receives dispatched recyclable batches from collection centers, verifies weights, and acknowledges raw material recovery.

Municipality Admin
System Governance

Full municipal oversight, worker assignment to pending requests, recovery rate statistics, coupon catalog management, and interactive charts.

Rewards & Coupons
Incentive Engine

Converts responsible waste segregation into tangible benefits: grocery discounts, bio-fertilizer vouchers, and municipal property tax rebates.
